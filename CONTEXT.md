# Research Taxonomy

This site presents Dingyi Zhuang's work through three career-facing research directions. A publication may belong to more than one direction.

## AI Agents and Foundation Models

Research on foundation-model systems that reason, use tools, improve through experience, and can be evaluated as end-to-end agents.

Secondary labels: LLM Agents; Tool Use & Planning; Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI); Multimodal Reasoning; Agent Evaluation; AI for Science.

## Intelligent Transportation and Autonomous Systems

AI methods and systems for transportation, urban mobility, and autonomous driving. This direction absorbs the site's former standalone “Spatiotemporal Data Modeling” theme.

Secondary labels: Spatiotemporal Learning; Forecasting & Optimization; Reinforcement Learning; Autonomous Driving.

## Trustworthy AI

Methods that make predictive and agentic AI reliable, calibrated, and equitable in real-world use.

Secondary labels: Uncertainty Quantification; Reliability & Calibration; Fairness.
